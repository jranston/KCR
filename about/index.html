About Kingston City Marathon

A running movement rooted in Kingston’s spirit, culture and commitment to making a difference.

Our story

A Movement Since 2013

Runners from Jamaica, the Caribbean, UK, Denmark, Japan, Canada, China, USA and beyond have joined us since our start in 2013.

The event debuted with a legendary start—Bart Yasso, the “Chief Running Officer” of Runner’s World, took part in our first half marathon. By 2019, we hosted over 5,000 runners!

The route puts Kingston’s culture, landmarks, and energy on display, with ample spots for supporters and unforgettable city views.

Pirates versus Heroes · 10th Staging

This year, Kingston City Marathon celebrates its 10th staging with the theme Pirates versus Heroes! The event will be staged across the weekend of March 12–14, 2027.

Prizes for best costumes. Spectators: line the streets and cheer, all against the backdrop of the Blue & John Crow Mountains, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Kingston is a UNESCO City of Music—the birthplace of Ska, Rocksteady, Dub, Reggae, and Dancehall.

Who we are

Changing lives, one step at a time.

Kingston City Run was founded in 2013 by Nicola Madden-Greig and Christopher Jarrett to help uplift Kingston and support our most vulnerable—through an event that promotes health, wellness, and the city’s best spirit. Geared to promote sport tourism, this event is perfect for travellers seeking to make a sustainable impact while having fun!

Organised by the Jamaica Hotel & Tourist Association (Kingston Chapter), powered by local sponsors, passionate volunteers, and a thriving community.
