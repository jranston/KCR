Volunteer in 2027

It takes over 500 volunteers to bring Kingston City Marathon, Half Marathon, 10K, 5K and the associated events to life.

Be part of the event crew

Individuals, schools, companies, community groups and organisations are invited to lend their support.

Race Kit & Expo

Support Race Kit Pickup and help participants get ready for race weekend.

Course Support

Serve as course spotters, support runners and help at aid stations.

Finish Line

Distribute finisher medals and assist in the areas vital to a successful event.

Volunteer requirements: Volunteers must be 14 or older. Minors must be accompanied by an adult. Group leaders with more than 10 volunteers are encouraged to contact the volunteer coordinator directly.
Groups & Schools

Volunteer together

Volunteer groups of 25 helpers or more may be eligible for a donation. Groups of any size are welcome.

High school and tertiary students can use their participation for community service and/or internship requirements. Please advise us in advance so participation forms and hours can be accounted for correctly.

Volunteer Perks

Depending on your volunteer hours, participants can earn perks while working for a great cause.
