Run for a Cause

Help Raise for Kingston’s Homeless

Running to rebuild the West after Hurricane Melissa and support those made homeless by economic, natural and man-made disasters.

Why we run

Your steps make an impact.

Every registration helps fund shelters, meals and hope for those who need it most. Kingston City Marathon is committed to fighting homelessness and supporting the most vulnerable.

Since 2013, over J$27 million has been raised for charities making a real difference in the city. Join us to help expand their impact.

2027 Fundraising Goal

J$10 Million

Help us make the 10th staging our biggest year of giving yet.

Charities You Help Support

Open Arms Drop-In Centre

Shelter, meals and life skills for Kingston’s homeless community.

Marie Atkins Night Shelter

A safe haven and support for hundreds each year.

Alpha Boys School

Education and hope for at-risk youth and future musicians.

Missionaries of the Poor

Caring for the homeless, abandoned and destitute with love and dignity.
