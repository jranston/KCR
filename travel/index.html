Spectator Info

Cheer them on

Watching a friend or family member running in the Kingston City Marathon & Half is easier than you think. Your runner will follow the course while you take shortcuts to get to your next viewing location before your runner does.

Make your own unique signs that your runner can easily identify. Cheer sign-making stations will be available at the Expo.

Know your marathoner's expected pace per mile. Determine when to be at the Cheer Zone and back at the finish line to catch your marathoner or half marathoner.

Attractions & Events

Turn race weekend into a Kingston getaway. Explore the capital's music, art, history, gardens and mountain experiences before or after you cross the finish line.

Bob Marley Museum

Step inside the reggae icon's former Hope Road home and recording headquarters, now a museum celebrating Bob Marley's life, music and enduring influence on Jamaica and the world.

Devon House

Discover one of Kingston's best-loved heritage landmarks, the 19th-century home of George Stiebel, with beautiful grounds, Jamaican dining, shopping and the famous Devon House ice cream.

Hope Botanical Gardens

Slow the pace among tropical palms, lawns and colourful plant collections at Kingston's historic botanical gardens, a peaceful green escape close to the city's eastern neighbourhoods.

National Gallery of Jamaica

Explore Jamaican and Caribbean creativity through the National Gallery's important collection of historic and contemporary art in Downtown Kingston.

Port Royal

Travel back to Jamaica's seafaring past in historic Port Royal, where Fort Charles and the old harbour community recall the city's famous stories of naval history, pirates and earthquakes.

Blue & John Crow Mountains

Head into the cool hills above Kingston for spectacular scenery, hiking, birdlife and the landscapes behind Jamaica's celebrated Blue Mountain coffee.

Craighton Estate

Experience Jamaica's coffee country at this historic estate in Irish Town, surrounded by lush mountain scenery and the cool climate of the Blue Mountain foothills.

Emancipation Park

Take a stroll through this landmark New Kingston park, a popular city oasis for walking, relaxing and enjoying landscaped gardens in the heart of the capital.

Make time beyond the race course and experience more of Kingston while you're here.
