Why join the adventure?

Four races. One unforgettable Kingston weekend.

🏁

Multiple Race Events

Take on the Marathon, Half Marathon, 10K run or join the accessible 5K Walk/Run/Wheel—open to all ages and abilities.

🏴‍☠️

Kingston’s Pirate’s Week

Happening on the same weekend! Also enjoy these festivities. A KCM Partner Event.

❤️

Help Raise for Charity

Proceeds support Kingston and Jamaica’s homeless charities and families. Target goal: J$10 million for our 10th staging!

👥

Team & Family Fun

Corporate challenges, family teams, and community groups are all welcome. Celebrate teamwork and community spirit.

🎓

Rep Your School Challenge

High school and tertiary students get a J$1,500 5K entry. Enter as a school team, compete for cash prizes, bragging rights, and pirate vibes.

🦸

Dress Up & Win Prizes

Unleash your inner pirate or superhero—compete in the costume contest for prizes and recognition.
