Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the Kingston City Marathon, Half Marathon, 10K and 5K.

When is Kingston City Marathon?

Kingston City Marathon: Marathon, Half Marathon, 10K and 5K is set for Sunday, March 14, 2027 at Emancipation Park, Kingston. The wider event weekend runs March 12–14, 2027.

How do I register?

Use the Register link in the menu and choose Local or International registration. Marathon and Half Marathon registration links are opening soon.

What race options are available?

You can choose from a Marathon, Half Marathon, 10K Run or a 5K Walk/Run/Wheel, suitable for all ages and abilities.

Where does the race start?

The event starts and finishes at Emancipation Park, Kingston.

Who benefits from this event?

Proceeds go towards charities supporting the homeless in Kingston and Jamaica, including Open Arms, Marie Atkins Night Shelter, Alpha Boys School and other community organisations.

Will I get a medal?

Yes, all registered participants will receive a commemorative Kingston City Marathon medal.

Is parking available?

Limited parking is available near Emancipation Park and nearby public parking is also available. We encourage carpooling or public transport where possible.

When and where do I collect my bib?

2027 collection dates and times are still being finalised. Pickup will be at the Jamaica Hotel & Tourist Association Head Office, 2 Ardenne Road, Kingston. There will be no race-morning bib pickup.

How can I get more information?

Visit the Contact page or email kingstoncityrun@gmail.com.
