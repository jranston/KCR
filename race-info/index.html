Course overview

Start / Finish: Emancipation Park

5K Route

Starts on Trafalgar Rd by NCB Atrium → right on Hope Rd → through Devon House Front Gate → exit at back and left on Kingsway → right on West Kings House Rd → through Kings House → exit East Kings House Rd → right to Lady Musgrave → down Lady Musgrave to Trafalgar → right, then left on Knutsford Boulevard → Emancipation Park finish.

10K Route

Starts on Trafalgar Rd by NCB Atrium → right on Hope Rd → through Devon House → Kingsway → West Kings House Rd → through Kings House → left at East Kings House Rd/Hope Road → up Hope Road past Bob Marley Museum → left Marley Road → right East Kings House Road → past Barbican Beach City → Paddington turnaround → reverse route to Hope Road → Lady Musgrave → Trafalgar → Knutsford Boulevard → Emancipation Park finish.
